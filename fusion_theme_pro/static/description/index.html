What's inside

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Six focused improvements that touch the parts of Odoo you use the most — the sidebar, menus, forms and lists.

Apps sidebar

A collapsible rail with instant search, pinned apps and full keyboard navigation. The active app is lit up — everything else stays quietly in the background.

Pin & hide apps

Pin your favorites to the top for one-click access. Hide the apps you never open — they collect under a tidy "Hidden apps" list at the bottom and return with a single click.

Menu icon styles

Keep the original Odoo icons, or switch to crisp outline icons, colorful tiles or a text-only layout — straight from Settings.

Form states

A stage stepper shows completed, current and upcoming stages at a glance. Editable records get an accent edge, while required, invalid and focused fields each have clear, unmistakable states.

List view

Calm headers, refined hover and selection states with an accent bar, inline-edit highlighting, grouped rows and a comfortable compact density.

Make it yours

Open the appearance panel in the top bar: light, dark or auto mode, seven accent colors, density and corner style. Administrators can lock in company defaults for everyone.
